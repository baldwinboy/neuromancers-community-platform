# Whereby

Whereby is one of the two selectable meeting providers
(`IntegrationSettings.meeting_provider = whereby`). The vendored client is in
`whereby/` (generated by clientele; see its `MANIFEST.md`).

## Rooms

`WherebyProvider.create_room` creates a transient room between `start_at` and an
hour after `end_at`, passing the configured room prefix. The result stores
`room_url` (guest link) and `meeting_id` as `provider_room_id` on the session.

## Join links

- **Guest:** the stored `meeting_link` (`roomUrl`).
- **Host:** `WherebyProvider.join_url` calls `GET /meetings/{id}?fields=[hostRoomUrl]`
  and caches the result for 60 seconds (`HOST_LINK_CACHE_SECONDS`). The provider
  join endpoint is only reachable inside the join window.

## Webhooks

`POST /webhooks/whereby/` with the `X-Whereby-Signature` HMAC-SHA256 header and
`IntegrationSettings.whereby_webhook_secret`. The handler
(`core/views/provider_webhooks.py`) accepts `data` or top-level fields, maps
`meetingId`/`meeting_id`/`roomName` to `provider_room_id`, and records:

- minutes from `startDate`/`endDate` (or `duration`),
- attendance from a participant `role` (`host`/`viewer`) or `isHost`.

Delivery is idempotent (activity key + session ledger upsert). The exact upstream
payload/header names are `UNVERIFIED` until a live webhook is observed; the
parser tolerates common variants.
